Those designs differ in CSS alone; if they each wrote their own markup, "ATS safe" would quietly come to mean something slightly different in each of them. The conventions encoded here, and why: * ONE COLUMN, in source order. Parsers read the document linearly. Nothing here is laid out in table cells, floated into a sidebar, or reordered by CSS, so extracted text arrives in the order a human reads it. * ROLE AND EMPLOYER ON ONE LINE, "Senior Engineer — TechScale Solutions". Most parsers look for the pair adjacent; stacking them onto separate lines is a common reason an employer goes unread. * STANDARD HEADINGS in real

elements, one per section. * NO TEXT IN DECORATION.
